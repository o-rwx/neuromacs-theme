# Neuro-Ergo Emacs Theme

A scientifically designed Emacs color theme built from the ground up to mitigate visual fatigue (asthenopia) and neurological exhaustion for professionals and academics facing extreme screen time (6-9 hours daily) of reading, writing, and coding.

## The Problem: Why Traditional Themes Fail
Standard color themes are designed for aesthetic appeal, not biological efficiency. Over prolonged periods, they trigger two major physiological stressors:
1. **Ciliary Muscle Fatigue:** High-saturation syntax highlighting (pure reds, blues, greens) causes **chromatic aberration**. Different colors refract at different angles on the retina, forcing the eye to constantly micro-refocus as you scan through lines of code or text.
2. **Pupil Sphincter Exhaustion & Halation:** Pure black backgrounds (`#000000`) paired with bright text cause the "halo effect." The extreme contrast causes light to bleed around the edges of the characters, forcing the visual cortex to work twice as hard to resolve the text shape.

## The Science Behind Neuro-Ergo

### 1. Perceptual Monochromacy (Magnocellular Optimization)
Instead of relying on a rainbow of colors to denote syntax, our monochromatic sepia variants rely entirely on **luminance and contrast**. This shifts the cognitive load away from the color-sensitive *parvocellular pathway* and heavily engages the *magnocellular pathway*, which is color-blind but highly responsive to depth and light intensity. You identify variables, functions, and keywords not by their hue, but by their perceived "weight" on the screen.

### 2. Elimination of the Halo Effect
- **Photopic (Day/High-Light):** Uses a "Pollen Paper" (`#e6d9c3`) base instead of pure white. This simulates the natural albedo of physical paper, cutting the sharp peaks of blue light and preventing photoreceptor overstimulation.
- **Mesopic (Night/Low-Light):** Uses a "Roasted Coffee" (`#282421`) base instead of absolute black. This prevents aggressive pupil dilation and completely eliminates light bleeding from characters.

### 3. Circadian Rhythm Protection (ipRGCs Mitigation)
The night/mesopic palettes are specifically engineered to avoid triggering the intrinsically photosensitive retinal ganglion cells (ipRGCs). By replacing short-wavelength (blue/white) emissions with amber, sand, and deep sepia tones, the theme ensures that melatonin production is not inhibited during late-night coding or reading sessions.

## Palettes Included
- **Neuro Photopic:** Low-saturation colors over a papyrus base.
- **Neuro Mesopic:** Desaturated cool/warm tones over a charcoal base.
- **Neuro Sepia (Monochromatic Light):** Pure luminance scaling over warm pollen paper.
- **Neuro Sepia (Monochromatic Dark):** Pure luminance scaling of sand photons over deep brown.

## Golden Rule of Usage
A theme can only do so much if your hardware is poorly calibrated. **Always perform the White Paper Test**: Hold a blank sheet of white paper next to your monitor under your current room lighting. Adjust your screen brightness until the white background of your editor emits the exact same amount of light as the paper reflects.
